Daggerheart Consumable Research Rules

To research a recipe for a specific consumable:

When you begin researching, start a project countdown. The countdown's starting value is based on the consumable's rarity:
(use the consumables table on p 132-133)

Roll ValueCountdown Value
1-121
13-242
25-363
37-484
49-605

Any time you use the Work On Project long rest move towards this project, you advance the countdown.
When the countdown reaches 0, you may make a Knowledge Reaction Roll. The base difficulty is the item's roll value from the consumables table. If the roll fails, the process must begin again from the start.

Optionally you may continue your research. Each additional time you use the Work On Project long rest move you add a 1d6 bonus to your eventual roll. These bonuses are cululative, so keep track of them as you go.

Example:

Lucien the Enchanter want to create a recipe for a Major Enlightenment Potion. The roll value of the Major Enlightenment Potion is 30, so the countdown will start at 3. Once Lucien has spent three long rest moves working towards this project he is able to make a roll, but his Knowledge is only +4.

He decides to spend a few more rests on research. After his first extra move he rolls 1d6 and gets a 3, so he writes down this value and presses on. The second extra move yields a 6 for a total bonus of +9. He decides to be extra careful and continues his research for another long rest, getting a 5 on his third roll yielding a +14 to his roll.

He finally decides to try for the recipe. Lucien needs a 30 to succeed. He rolls his Knowledge Reaction Roll getting a 7 on the Hope die and a 9 on the Fear die (but this is a reaction roll, so there is no hope or fear generated), added to his +4 knowledge and the +14 from the extended research yields him a total of 34 which exceeds the 30 difficulty. Lucien now has a recipe for a Major Enlightenment Potion.

Now as a downtime move Lucien can create his new potion.
