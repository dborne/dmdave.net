Here are some low(ish) power magic items for low-level parties.
Kroquium's Tracker
A large, oval red gem is clasped in a gold fixture hung from a slim golden chain.
Upon use of the command word, the gem comes loose from its fitting. While the gem is detached, the necklace cannot be removed from the wielder (except by remove curse or similar magics). The wearer is keenly (even to the level of distraction) aware of the gem's location. The wearer will have disadvantage on notice, initiative and concentration checks. The wearer will know the direction and distance of the gem anywhere on the same plane, and will be aware of it moving to a different plane (and, on a successful dc 15 arcana check, will be able to identify the plane it moved to)
Re-attaching the gem can be ... problematic. The wearer will need to roll an arcane (dex) check to avoid issues. Roll whatever the largest die size that the check value matched or exceeded (e.g. rolled an 8 -> 1d8, rolled a 15 -> 1d12). On a 1 the entire amulet explodes, dealing 2d10 force damage to the wearer (no save). Anyone within 10' of the wearer may make a dex save (DC 12) for half damage.
Katz and Bartleby's Repeating Dart.
An unassuming dart with a silver body, a black point and woodpecker feather fletching.
Functions as a standard dart. When thrown, regardless of the result, it re-appears in the owners hand at the end of their next turn (guaranteed, or your money back).
Caution: Katz and Bartleby's Repeating Dart is not recommended for use outside the prime material plane, do not play catch with Katz and Bartleby's Repeating Dart, attempting to manually retrieve Katz and Bartleby's Repeating Dart before its return cycle has completed can lead to unexpected phenomena and will void all warranties.

Recondite Redemption Ring.
A ring of pure hematite, cool to the touch. It fits well, despite not appearing able to at first.
The wearer, upon falling unconsious, is rendered invisible for 4 turns and teleported to a random "safe" location within 60 feet.
Warning: Whirlwind Magical Items is not responsible if wearer forgets to remove ring before going to bed.
